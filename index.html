Our mission

Opening doors to AI for every Somali woman.

We want more women to participate in the AI conversation—not only as users of technology, but as creators, researchers, leaders, and innovators.

Our vision

A future where Somali women have the knowledge, confidence, networks, and opportunities to contribute to responsible artificial intelligence.

Our values

Inclusion · Curiosity · Collaboration · Responsible innovation
